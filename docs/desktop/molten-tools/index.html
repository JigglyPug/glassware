Molten Tools

Molten glass and quality-of-life fixes for YouTube.

Molten Tools gives YouTube and YouTube Music a molten-glass lava background, plus a set of quality-of-life fixes. Click the orange lava button in the top bar for settings.

YouTube, YouTube MusicDesktopv3.7.3
Install Molten ToolsView source
Features

A lava lamp behind every video

A living background taken from the video or album art, with Calm, User and Crazy profiles that each keep their own look.

Never "Still watching?" again

Answers "Continue watching?" and "Video paused" prompts so playback keeps going.

Colors from the art

The title bar and seek bar take their color from what's playing.

Your quality, every time

Pick a resolution once, with separate choices for high-frame-rate videos and YouTube Music, plus theater mode.

Screenshot safe

Hides your account and channel names, handles and email everywhere on the site.

Default account

Opens YouTube on the account or channel you choose.

Tuned for your GPU

Frame rate, sample size and blob switches keep the lava light on any machine.
