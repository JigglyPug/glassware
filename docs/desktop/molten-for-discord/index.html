Molten for Discord

Molten glass for Discord

Molten for Discord puts a lava-lamp background made from what you're looking at behind frosted-glass panes, and keeps your favorite channels one point away on the lava button.

Features

Lava from what you're looking at

The background comes from the image, video or stream in the middle of the chat, or the profile you open. With nothing on screen it takes the server's icon colors, or Discord purple.

Glass over the lava

The server list, channels, chat and member list turn to frosted glass, with Calm, User and Crazy profiles that each keep their own look.

Favorites on the lava button

Point at the lava button and your favorite channels, threads and DMs drip down from it. Reorder them, color them by server and see Recent in the panel.

Saved messages and reminders

Point at a message and click its lava drop to save it to a folder of your own, or to be reminded later, with a note saying why. Reminders you missed while Discord was closed show up when it opens.

Your activity, at a glance

Where your Discord time goes as a cluster of bubbles, one per server or DM: point at one and it swells, click it to go there. Daily time, messages and busy hours sit beside it, compared with your own history, and none of it leaves your browser.

Voice in the lava

In a call, the lava takes the avatar of whoever is talking.

Mentions and keywords that glow

Messages that mention you, or use words you pick, get a lava glow instead of a flat bar, with lava blobs around them if you like.

Colors from the art

The title bar and the video seek bar take their color from the lava. When nothing on screen gives a color, Prism takes the title bar back.

Screenshot safe and privacy blur

Hide your own name and avatar, and blur images, DMs, server icons or whole channels until you point at them.

Easy on your GPU

The lava pauses while you scroll and when Discord isn't the active window, and calms down during calls and streams.

What's new

1.0.4 2026-11-17 Lava around opened media (Lava tab, Colors; on by default): a picture or video opened full size sits in light glass over the lava, in the picture's own colors, instead of on a dark backdrop with the chat behind it. A video in fullscreen keeps the lava around it instead of black bars. Closing it brings the chat straight back.

1.0.3 2026-11-17 Lighter while a video or stream plays: only the lava's video follows the frame rate now. Keeping the button in place, cleaning the page, checking reminders and counting activity stay on their own clock, instead of running up to 60 times a second. Switched off, Molten stops watching the page entirely. Reminders: at the limit of 100, a new one never pushes out one that's due sooner (a finished reminder goes first, then the one furthest away). "In 1 month" from the 31st stops at the end of next month instead of spilling into the one after. Export CSV: a server or channel named like a spreadsheet formula starts with an apostrophe, so a spreadsheet shows it as text instead of running it. Export data leaves out the names Screenshot safe hides inside saved messages, reminders and notes too (they read "User"). Your data now says what a saved message keeps: a short preview (its first 80 characters), its author and a link.

1.0.2 2026-11-17 Your data in General, laid out like Hourglass's: Export CSV (your activity, a row per day and channel), Export data (settings, lava looks, favorites, saved messages, reminders, notes, server colors), Import data (adds what's in a backup to what you have; nothing is removed) and Erase activity, which asks first and waits for OK. Exports leave out the names Screenshot safe hides. Erasing activity moved here from the Activity tab. Mention blobs: every message gets its own blobs, in their own places, at their own speed, instead of a run of mentions playing the same animation side by side. The icon shows in Tampermonkey's list again: if it didn't download when you installed, this update fetches it.
