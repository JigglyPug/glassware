Molten for Discord

Molten glass for Discord.

Molten for Discord puts a lava-lamp background made from what you're looking at behind frosted-glass panes, and keeps your favorite channels one point away on the lava button.

Features

Lava from what you're looking at

The background comes from the image, video or stream in the middle of the chat, or the profile you open. With nothing on screen it takes the server's icon colors, or Discord purple.

Glass over the lava

The server list, channels, chat and member list turn to frosted glass, with Calm, User and Crazy profiles that each keep their own look.

Favorites on the lava button

Point at the lava button and your favorite channels, threads and DMs drip down from it. Reorder them, color them by server and see Recent in the panel.

Saved messages and reminders

Point at a message and click its lava drop to save it to a folder of your own, or to be reminded later, with a note saying why. Reminders you missed while Discord was closed show up when it opens. Anything you remove waits in the trash for 30 days, and done reminders stay there crossed off.

Your activity, at a glance

Where your Discord time goes as a cluster of bubbles, one per server or DM: point at one and it swells, click it to go there. Daily time, messages and busy hours sit beside it, compared with your own history, and none of it leaves your browser.

Voice in the lava

In a call, the lava takes the avatar of whoever is talking.

Mentions and keywords that glow

Messages that mention you, or use words you pick, get a lava glow instead of a flat bar, with lava blobs around them if you like.

Colors from the art

The title bar and the video seek bar take their color from the lava. When nothing on screen gives a color, Prism takes the title bar back.

Screenshot safe and privacy blur

Hide your own name and avatar, and blur images, DMs, server icons or whole channels until you point at them.

Easy on your GPU

The lava pauses while you scroll and when Discord isn't the active window, and calms down during calls and streams.

What's new

1.0.12 2026-11-21 Removing a favorite, a saved message, a folder or a reminder asks first, right in the row: Cancel or Move to trash. A trash can next to Channels, Messages and Reminders opens the trash. Put anything back, or delete it for good, which asks too. Empty trash clears it all at once. Reminders you mark done show up there too, crossed off, and can go back on your list. The trash and finished reminders clear on their own after 30 days.

1.0.11 2026-11-20 In Remind me, picking Tonight, Tomorrow or Next week turns the dial to match, and so does picking an exact day and time. Tonight is 8 PM, or 10 PM once 8 is close, and it's left out late in the evening, so it can never land after Tomorrow. Just after midnight, Tomorrow means the morning coming up. Lighter on busy channels: with no picture viewer open, new images no longer trigger a sweep of the whole page.

1.0.10 2026-11-19 The bubbles card says it the way you'd say it ("Lava Lounge took a third of your Discord time"), and "What changed?" puts a big jump in words ("more than halved"). The bubble key is in plain sentences, and the line under the bubbles shows the biggest one until you point at one. Settings descriptions say what happens when a switch is off in a sentence, and a few read more clearly. The last date under the day charts no longer drops onto a second line when the range ends in a part-week.
